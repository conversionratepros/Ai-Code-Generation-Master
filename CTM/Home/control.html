    About Us

    Launched in 1983, CTM is a household name in Southern Africa, enjoying strong brand affinity based on its reputation for a high-quality year-round value offering. Represented by 94 stores in Southern and East Africa, CTM is the largest specialist tile and bathroom retailer in the country.

    Today we are the leading specialist retailer in this market. With over 80 stores throughout South Africa and Africa, we buy more tiles and bathroomware products than any other retailer. This bulk buying power means that we offer a wider selection of products and pass the massive savings on to you.

    At CTM there are no middlemen. We work directly with designers and factories throughout the world to design exclusive brands that meet the highest standards. Tivoli Taps, Kilimanjaro, and Organic Earth tiles, Elf laminates, Promax tools, and ProGrip adhesives and grouts, bring you a level of quality, value and support that we are happy to guarantee.

    CTM stores nationwide are inspirational environments, filled with new and stimulating ideas. You'll find yourself at home and in the capable hands of well-trained and motivated staff. Our stores are run by the individual owners, ensuring an unmatched level of personal service.

    The CTM online store was started in 2012, and our drive was to assist all customers, local and abroad with home improvements and renovations from the comfort of their home or office. With a huge collection of great quality products, a variety of payment methods and a team of product experts ready to assist we are here to make your online experience with us a pleasant one.
