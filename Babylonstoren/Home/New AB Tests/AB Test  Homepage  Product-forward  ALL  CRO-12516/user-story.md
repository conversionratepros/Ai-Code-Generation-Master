5. **Single customer review** — quotation + reviewer name below.

   - Delivery: label "Delivery", heading "Free Delivery Over R500", delivery-areas text, button "Shop all"

10. **Customer reviews carousel** — heading "Loved by Families Across South Africa", supporting text; swipeable review cards (star rating, text, name, verified indicator); pagination indicators tracking active slide.
11. **Newsletter sign-up** — media, heading "Enjoy 5% Off Your First Order", supporting text, "Your Name" + "Your Email" inputs, "Sign Up" button, reassurance line below. Add whatever required inputs the form needs.
12. **Demote existing editorial content** below the shopping path. Footer unchanged.
