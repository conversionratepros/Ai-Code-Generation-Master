Who we are

The London Dental Institute delivers flexible, fully online postgraduate education for dentists around the world. Our UK-accredited programmes combine expert-led teaching, interactive modules and clinical simulation kits, helping dentists build confidence, advance their clinical skills and treat more cases in-house. Through a global network of dentists and faculty, we make postgraduate dental education accessible to working clinicians wherever they practice.

Accreditation

Recognised, accredited dental education

Our diplomas are accredited by EduQual (Level 7), meeting the quality standards required to provide a globally recognised qualification. LDI is also an institutional member of the Association for Dental Education in Europe, working to raise standards and bring dental education closer together across the region.
