Qualifications that expand what you treat

Diploma in Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopaedics

Treat the orthodontic cases you would usually refer out, from clear aligners to fixed and removable appliances. Covers assessment and treatment planning, digital orthodontics and TADS, with hands-on exercises to support your practical development. 12-month part-time online programme.

Diploma in Aesthetic & Restorative Dentistry

Offer the aesthetic and restorative treatments your patients currently go elsewhere for, from direct and indirect restorations to smile design and occlusion. Covers bonding and isolation, shade analysis, biomimetic and digital dentistry, with guided practical exercises. 12-month part-time online programme.

Diploma in Dental Implantology & Oral Surgery

Place implants and carry out oral surgery in your own practice, from single units through to bone grafting and full-arch restoration. Covers surgical assessment, implant planning, soft-tissue management, exodontia and dental trauma, with hands-on practical training. 12-month part-time online programme.

Accreditation

Recognised, accredited dental education

Our diplomas are accredited by EduQual (Level 7), meeting the quality standards required to provide a globally recognised qualification. LDI is also an institutional member of the Association for Dental Education in Europe, working to raise standards and bring dental education closer together across the region.
