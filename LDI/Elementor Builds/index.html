You're already seeing these patients.

The worn anteriors. The discoloured composites that have never quite matched. The patient who points at their smile and says can we do something about all of this?

You can treat them. But often, you only treat part of it. Perhaps just a single tooth when all anteriors need addressing. Whitening when more intervention was justifiable. Something very conservative when the patient was ready for something more comprehensive.

It's not about ability. It's that aesthetic cases don't arrive with a clear framework the way simpler restorative ones do — no agreed sequence for full-arch planning, no structured approach to reading occlusion into a smile case, no formal training in how to build and present a treatment plan that genuinely addresses what the patient came in for.

So you do what you can confidently justify. And the patient leaves with something, rather than everything they needed, or wanted.

Build the clinical framework to treat aesthetic cases in full

Over 12 months, you build the clinical framework to assess, plan, and deliver aesthetic cases in full — with the confidence to propose treatment that properly meets what your patients are asking for.

  • Develop a structured approach to full-arch aesthetic assessment

    so you can see and plan the complete case, not just the presenting complaint

  • Build confidence in treatment sequencing

    when to whiten first, when indirect restorations are the right call, how occlusion shapes every aesthetic decision

  • Present comprehensive treatment plans patients understand and accept

    and charge accordingly

  • Deliver high-quality composite artistry, indirect restorations, and more

    with 1:1 clinical support from expert faculty

  • Build on a UK-accredited, evidence-based foundation

    not short courses with no follow-up

Can you really learn Aesthetic & Restorative Dentistry online?

The programme combines online theory with a comprehensive Clinical Simulation Kit and 1:1 case support from our expert tutors. You receive the models and equipment needed to practise hands-on techniques at home, alongside live Zoom study clubs and optional in-person workshops in London.

  • Clinical Simulation Kit

    models and equipment for composite, indirect restoration, and clinical photography practice, shipped to you

  • 1:1 clinical case support

    apply new techniques to your own patients with tutor feedback through the VLE

  • Monthly Live Study Clubs

    live Zoom sessions with faculty, with recordings on the VLE if you miss them

  • Optional in-person workshops at LDI London

    additional hands-on teaching for those who can travel (not required)
